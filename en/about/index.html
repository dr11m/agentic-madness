About Agentic Madness

I write about AI models, the agents that use them, and the tools, skills and protocols involved in working with them.

I test and compare tools and approaches, run experiments, and share what I've learned from using them in my work: what works, what doesn't, and why.

My background

I work in data analysis. I've been working with time series for over four years, solving different kinds of problems in different fields. I also build and maintain my own projects.

I've been using AI agents since they first became widely available. I try out almost anything that interests me. It's more than a hobby: agents are now a regular part of my work.

My approach

I base my articles on facts about the tools and results from experiments. I also share my experience and opinions, but make it clear which statements are facts and which are my own views.

I want to understand what models and agents are useful for, where their limits are, and which claims are just marketing. I don't want to exaggerate their abilities or dismiss what they can do.

Join the discussion

I'd like to hear about your experience and compare notes, especially when we reach different conclusions. You can find updates, notes and discussions on Telegram. The channel is in Russian.
